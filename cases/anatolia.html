Customer Overview

Company Background

Anatolia SecureScan Technologies is a Turkey-based technology company specializing in ID document scanners, identity verification terminals, and access control devices. Its solutions are widely deployed in hotels, office buildings, industrial parks, border-adjacent logistics hubs, and regulated commercial facilities.

Target Market

Hotels and hospitality chains, commercial buildings and office parks, industrial zones and logistics facilities, private security service providers, and government-adjacent regulated commercial facilities requiring real-time identity validation and data reporting.

Product Requirements

Reliable cellular connectivity across Turkey, local network compliance without permanent roaming, cost-efficient connectivity for large-scale deployments, centralized device and SIM management, and stable data transmission for ID scan uploads and verification requests.

Digital Transformation

Upgrading traditional standalone ID scanners into cellular-connected smart terminals, enabling centralized device management, real-time data synchronization, remote diagnostics, and cloud-based identity verification services across dispersed locations.

Business & Operational Challenges

📜

Regulatory Compliance Requirements

Regulatory restrictions in Turkey prohibit permanent roaming, requiring fully local cellular connectivity solutions with Turkish SIM cards while ensuring compliance with telecommunications regulations and avoiding international roaming penalties.

💰

Cost Optimization Pressure

High cost sensitivity in the Turkish market with strong pricing pressure from local operators such as Vodafone Turkey and Turkcell, requiring careful balance between connectivity reliability, data plan optimization, and competitive total cost of ownership.

📊

Distributed Terminal Management

Operational complexity in managing thousands of ID scanning terminals distributed across hotels, office buildings, and remote facilities nationwide, requiring centralized monitoring, remote diagnostics, and efficient support operations to minimize site visits.

⚖️

Reliability vs. Cost Balance

Balancing connectivity reliability, regulatory compliance, and cost efficiency while maintaining high availability for critical identity verification operations in hospitality and security applications where downtime impacts customer experience and compliance.
