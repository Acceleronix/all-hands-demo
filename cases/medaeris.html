Customer Overview

Company Background

MedAeris HealthTech is a European medical technology company specializing in respiratory therapy devices and chronic care management solutions. Its product portfolio includes connected ventilators, oxygen concentrators, and remote monitoring equipment used in both homecare and clinical environments.

Target Market

Home-use respiratory therapy device users in Europe and overseas markets, hospitals and telemedicine service providers, and medical device leasing and homecare service operators seeking connected healthcare solutions.

Product Requirements

Requires reliable multi-country cellular connectivity, long lifecycle and low-power connectivity for medical devices, centralized device and connectivity management, and high standards for data security, reliability, and regulatory compliance.

Digital Healthcare Strategy

Transforming traditional medical devices into cellular-connected smart medical terminals, enabling remote device monitoring, predictive maintenance, and long-term patient management through cloud-based platforms.

Business Challenges to Address

⏱️

Long Device Lifecycle Management

Medical devices have 10+ year lifecycles requiring highly stable and sustainable connectivity solutions that can adapt to evolving cellular network technologies and carrier landscape changes over extended deployment periods.

🌍

Multi-Country Compliance

Complex connectivity and regulatory compliance requirements across multiple European countries and global markets, each with different medical device regulations, data privacy laws, and cellular network standards.

📊

Distributed Device Operations

Operational inefficiency caused by geographically distributed devices across homecare and clinical settings, requiring centralized management, remote diagnostics, and predictive maintenance capabilities to reduce site visits.

🔐

Healthcare Data Security

Strict regulatory and data privacy requirements in the healthcare sector including GDPR, HIPAA, and medical device-specific regulations requiring end-to-end security, audit trails, and compliance reporting.
