Customer Overview

Company Background

EuroDrive Telematics is a Europe-based technology company delivering telematics and asset tracking solutions through an MVNO + telematics platform business model. The company provides end-to-end connectivity and data services for fleet operators, logistics companies, and industry customers.

Target Market

Commercial vehicle and logistics fleet operators, vehicle rental and shared mobility providers, heavy equipment and asset tracking customers, and automotive OEMs and aftermarket telematics providers across European markets.

Product Requirements

Unified cellular connectivity across multiple countries and networks, scalable SIM/eSIM management for large device fleets, support for high-frequency data transmission and real-time tracking, and flexible data plans with billing capabilities for MVNO operations.

Service Operations

With operations across Europe and neighboring regions, EuroDrive enables real-time vehicle tracking, operational monitoring, and remote management for large-scale connected vehicle deployments with comprehensive telematics analytics.
